# Scanned (image-only) PDF — MinerU 4.1.3 vs Qwen3.5-0.8B

Archetype: `pdf-scanned` · Date: 2026-11-01 · Protocol: [METHOD](../METHOD.md)

Same pairing as the [academic record](../pdf-academic/2026-11-01_mineru41-vs-qwen35-08b.md); document here is a 6-page image-only paper scan (no text layer) — the OCR stress path.

## Results

- **MinerU wins 2/2 position-swapped rounds, all four dimensions** (text/table/formula/order).
- VLM-route defects cited by the judge: an entire Background section dropped, formulas missing normalization denominators, table footnotes lost, garbled email brackets.
- Speed: Qwen3.5-0.8B 4.3–9.6 s/page on this set; MinerU GPU cold 92s/6 pages (15.7s inference = 0.38 page/s — slower per page than the text-layer PDF, reflecting the OCR workload).

## Note

Scanned documents widen the gap: the pipeline's OCR-det/rec path carries what a general VLM does in one forward pass with less redundancy — a dropped glance is a dropped section.
